# Green Leaf Café – Staff & Customer Handbook (SAMPLE DATA)

> This is made-up sample data for testing the AI Chat app. Green Leaf Café is a fictional business.

## About us
Green Leaf Café opened in March 2021 at 12 Nguyen Hue Street, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City.
We have a second branch at 45 Tran Phu Street, Hai Chau District, Da Nang, opened in August 2023.
Our hotline is 1900 6868 and our email is hello@greenleaf.example.

## Opening hours
- Monday to Friday: 7:00 – 22:00
- Saturday and Sunday: 7:30 – 23:00
- Public holidays (Tet): closed on the first three days of the Lunar New Year.
The kitchen stops taking food orders 30 minutes before closing time. Drinks are served until closing.

## Wi-Fi
Network name: GreenLeaf_Guest. The password changes every Monday and is printed on the receipt.
Wi-Fi is free for customers for 3 hours per visit.

## Loyalty program
- Customers earn 1 point for every 10,000 VND spent.
- 100 points = one free drink of size M.
- Members get a free birthday drink (any size) during their birthday month; show an ID card.
- Points expire 12 months after they are earned.
- Silver members (500 points earned in a year) get 5% off all orders. Gold members (1,500 points in a year) get 10% off.

## Payment
We accept cash, Visa, Mastercard, MoMo, ZaloPay and VNPay QR. We do not accept checks.
Orders over 2,000,000 VND can ask for a company VAT invoice; the invoice must be requested on the same day.

## Delivery
Delivery is available through GrabFood and ShopeeFood within 5 km of each branch.
Free delivery for orders over 300,000 VND when ordering by the hotline.

## Refunds and complaints
If a drink is wrong or not satisfying, we remake it for free within 15 minutes of purchase.
Refunds are given only when an item is out of stock after payment.
Complaints can be sent to hello@greenleaf.example; we reply within 2 working days.

## Staff rules
- Shifts: morning 6:30–14:30, evening 14:00–22:30. Weekend shifts end at 23:30.
- Staff must arrive 10 minutes before the shift to change into uniform.
- Each staff member gets one free drink per shift and 30% off food.
- Shift swaps must be approved by the store manager at least 24 hours in advance.
- Hourly wage for new part-time staff: 25,000 VND/hour, rising to 28,000 VND/hour after 6 months.
